Methodology & interpretation

Dataset: CHIRPS daily precipitation, datatype 0.

Spatial statistic: Average rainfall across the selected polygon for each daily time step.

Accumulation: Sum of the daily area-average rainfall values over the selected date range.

Units: Millimetres (mm).

Important: These are historical rainfall estimates, not a future forecast. The area-average rainfall is not a measurement at every location within the selected boundary.
