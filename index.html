07 / METHODOLOGY & DATA NOTES

Zonal statistics are calculated by testing raster pixel centres against each Enumeration Area polygon. Mean, maximum, minimum, standard deviation, sum and valid pixel count are calculated from valid raster values.

The current browser implementation assumes a north-up geographic raster in EPSG:4326. Projected, rotated or differently referenced rasters must first be reprojected to EPSG:4326.

Results depend on the uploaded raster product, band, scale, offset, no-data value and temporal composite. They should not be interpreted as electricity consumption or direct measurements of population.
