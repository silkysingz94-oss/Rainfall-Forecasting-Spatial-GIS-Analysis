About this map

A respectful, community-led map for recording places of remembrance across The Gambia. It begins with no preloaded memorial claims or visitor totals; locations may be added locally or loaded from GeoJSON.

Entries are user-provided and are not independently verified or officially endorsed. Please obtain consent and verify details before sharing memorial information.
